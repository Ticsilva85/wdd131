Study Planner — Project Profile

Study Planner is a personal web development project created as part of my studies at BYU-Pathway Worldwide, particularly within the Web Development Fundamentals course. The project combines my academic learning with my personal interest in education, technology, and productivity. As an English teacher, I understand how important organization, consistency, and good study habits are for students. At the same time, as a student myself, I have experienced the challenges of balancing work, teaching, personal responsibilities, and academic goals. These experiences inspired me to create a website that encourages students to organize their studies and develop more effective learning habits.

Purpose

The main purpose of Study Planner is to help students plan, organize, and manage their study routines. The website provides a simple and accessible environment where users can think about their goals, organize their tasks, maintain focus, and develop a more consistent approach to studying. Rather than being only a task-management tool, Study Planner is designed around the idea that successful learning requires planning, focus, persistence, and organization.

Academic and Personal Development

This project represents more than a BYU assignment. It is also an opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills I am developing in web development to a project connected to my professional interests. Through Study Planner, I have been practicing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, CSS Grid, Flexbox, and user interface design. I have also learned how important it is to test, revise, and improve a website based on usability and different screen. The project connects my background in English teaching and bilingual education with my growing interest in technology and front-end development. My goal is to continue developing these skills and eventually create educational tools that can have a practical impact on students and teachers.

Vision

My vision for Study Planner is to create a useful, simple, and motivating digital space for students. In the future, the project could evolve to include more interactive tools, personalized study routines, progress tracking, educational resources, and other features that support independent learning.
