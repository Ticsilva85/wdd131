Website Planning Document – Study Planner

Site Name

The name clearly describes the purpose of the website. It is simple, easy to remember, and immediately tells users that the application is designed to help them organize and manage their study schedule.

Site Purpose

Study Planner is a responsive web application that helps students organize their study sessions, manage tasks, and track their learning progress. Users can create study tasks, assign priorities, mark them as completed, and monitor their overall progress through a simple and intuitive interface.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

A college student wants to organize their weekly study schedule before exams.

  • How can I add my study sessions for each subject?
  • How can I prioritize the most important tasks?

Scenario 2

A student wants to monitor their productivity throughout the week.

  • How many study tasks have I completed?
  • What study sessions are still pending?

Color Schema

Color Hex Code Purpose

Light Gray #F4F4F4 Page background

Typography

Font Purpose
Poppins, sans-serif Used for navigation, headings and titles to provide a modern and clean look.
Roboto, sans-serif Used for body text to ensure readability and a comfortable reading experience, including: paragraphs, lists, and form labels
